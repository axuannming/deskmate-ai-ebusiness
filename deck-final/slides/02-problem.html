Missed enquiries answered for 82% less than part-time reception.

Problem & Business Model
B2B Fee-for-Service

The problem

Independent Hong Kong salons (1 to 10 staff) rarely employ a full-time receptionist. While therapists are with clients or after closing, WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries go unanswered and customers book elsewhere.

B2B Fee-for-Service model

Salons pay a predictable monthly subscription with no per-booking commission. A time-limited 14-day trial, distinct from a permanent freemium tier, comes with done-for-you menu onboarding.
