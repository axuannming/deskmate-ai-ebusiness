Slide 1 (0:00–0:10): Good morning. DeskMate AI is a bilingual AI receptionist that answers WhatsApp, Instagram and website enquiries for small Hong Kong beauty salons.
Slide 2 (0:10–0:40): Salons with one to ten staff rarely have a receptionist, so enquiries are missed during treatments and after closing. At minimum wage, four hours of reception a day costs about 4,482 Hong Kong dollars a month; our Pro plan costs 788, which is 82% less. This is B2B e-commerce on a Fee-for-Service model. Salons pay a predictable monthly fee with no per-booking commission, and the 14-day trial is time limited, so it is not a freemium tier.
Slide 3 (0:40–1:05): We target owner-run salons in Mong Kok, Causeway Bay and Tsim Sha Tsui. As a new brand, we focus on the awareness and exploration stages of the customer relationship life cycle: social and search ads inform owners, then the web demo, the trial and a one-to-one demo explain the product. Our targets are 25% trial-to-paid conversion and under 5% monthly churn.
Slide 4 (1:05–1:30): All three channels feed one booking engine and one shared calendar. It runs on a three-tier design with managed cloud and serverless functions, so we pay per use and scale for evening peaks, while a CDN keeps pages fast on mobile. Salons pay by recurring card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or FPS for annual invoices.
Slide 5 (1:30–2:30 Live Demo): Here is the live Wix prototype. The navigation is clear and the layout works on mobile. In the booking demo, I type a salon name, Lumière Nail Studio. A client asks in Cantonese for a gel manicure after six, DeskMate AI replies in that salon's name and books six-thirty, and the shared calendar marks the slot. On Pricing, each plan starts with a 14-day trial; I'll click Start Pro 14-day trial, and the simulated checkout shows HK$0 due today. Finally, the Book a Demo form is our one-to-one touchpoint, with an unticked opt-in box and a PDPO notice.
Slide 6 (2:30–3:00): We are asking for 400,000 Hong Kong dollars in angel or seed funding, estimated with total cost of ownership. Ongoing costs are 122% of the initial build, inside the textbook's 50 to 200% range. We cover monthly operating costs at 24 Pro salons and aim for 60 by month twelve, or 47,280 dollars a month if all choose Pro. Thank you.
