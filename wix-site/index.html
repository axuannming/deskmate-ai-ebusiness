    Frequently asked questions.

    Clear answers for Hong Kong salon owners comparing DeskMate AI with general messaging tools.

    Meta Business Agent already answers WhatsApp. Why pay for DeskMate AI?

    General tools leave setup to the salon owner and usually cover one channel. We set up your Hong Kong treatment menu, prices and hours for you, and DeskMate AI keeps WhatsApp, Instagram DM and website chat on one calendar.

    How does the 14-day free trial work?

    You pay HK$0 today. We configure your salon's services and opening hours so you can test the full booking workflow with real enquiries for 14 days. If you decide not to continue before Day 14, cancel with zero charge.

    What happens when a customer asks a complex or personal question?

    DeskMate AI handles routine pricing, availability, booking and rescheduling automatically. Whenever a customer mentions allergies, custom bridal packages or requests a human, it flags and summarises the conversation directly to the owner's WhatsApp.

    How do you protect salon and customer booking data in Hong Kong?

    We follow Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO) data minimisation principles. We store only the booking details needed to confirm appointments and send reminders, on encrypted managed cloud infrastructure.
